# Denial Workflow Map

```text
Patient Scheduled
      |
      v
Insurance Collected
      |
      v
Eligibility Verified
      |
      v
Authorization Requirement Checked
      |
      v
Clinical Documentation Completed
      |
      v
Claim Submitted
      |
      v
Payer Response Received
      |
      v
Denial Identified
      |
      v
Root Cause Traced Back Upstream
      |
      v
Workflow Fix Added Before Next Claim
```

## Key Workflow Principle

The denial should not be treated as the starting point. It should be treated as the signal that something earlier in the workflow failed.

## Upstream Checkpoints

- Intake accuracy
- Eligibility confirmation
- Referral requirement review
- Authorization requirement review
- Medical necessity documentation
- Claim readiness review

## Downstream Effects

- Delayed reimbursement
- More manual rework
- Higher A/R volume
- Patient billing confusion
- Staff frustration
- Lower workflow trust
